# Privacy Policy

Read stores account identity, profile role, uploaded manuscripts, reading purchases, moderation decisions, reports, and policy acceptance records needed to operate the service. Authentication is provided through Firebase when configured.

Uploaded manuscripts and unpublished books are restricted to their publisher and administrators. Published books and covers are public inside Read. Reports are visible to administrators and are used for safety and moderation.

Read does not sell personal information. Operational providers may process data to provide hosting, authentication, text-to-speech, and other product features. Contact the operator to request access, correction, or deletion where applicable.
