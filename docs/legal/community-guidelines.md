# Community Guidelines

Read welcomes original books and thoughtful participation. Do not publish or report content in bad faith.

Prohibited content includes copyright infringement, targeted abuse, exploitation, unlawful material, spam, deceptive metadata, malware, and content intended to evade moderation or payment controls.

Reports should identify a genuine issue. Administrators may dismiss reports, resolve them without action, temporarily hide a book, or permanently remove it. Authors receive clear rejection or visibility notes when appropriate.
